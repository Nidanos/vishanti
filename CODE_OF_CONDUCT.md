# Code of conduct

Vishanti is for learners, many of them beginners, and its community should feel the same: welcoming, patient and respectful.

- Be kind. Assume good faith, and explain rather than dismiss.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, or unwelcome personal attention, in any project space.

We follow the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Report a problem to **hello@nidanos.com**. Reports are kept confidential, and the maintainers may warn, suspend or remove anyone who breaks these rules.
